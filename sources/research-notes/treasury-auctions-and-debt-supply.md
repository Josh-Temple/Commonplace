# Treasury auctions and debt supply — research note

## Purpose

Support official issuance and auction procedures while bounding practitioner interpretation of results.

## Evidence record

- **TreasuryDirect — How Auctions Work** — U.S. Treasury. https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/how-auctions-work/ — Competitive/noncompetitive process and awards. Limitation: not a market-direction model.
- **Auction Announcements, Data & Results** — U.S. Treasury. https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/announcements-data-results/ — Official terms and result fields.
- **Treasury Quarterly Refunding** — U.S. Treasury. https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/quarterly-refunding — Financing announcements. Limitation: later market attribution remains interpretive.
- **Primary Dealers** — Federal Reserve Bank of New York. https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/primarydealers — Dealer role and list. Limitation: does not prove dealer capacity causes yield moves.
