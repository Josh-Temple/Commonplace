# Cross-asset confirmation and divergence — research note

## Purpose

Bound the use of correlation, confirmation, divergence, and lead/lag in macro interpretation.

## Evidence record

- **Correlation and causation in economics** — General empirical-method principle; companion official series notes provide instrument definitions. Correlation does not identify a causal channel without assumptions.
- **Discount Rates** — John H. Cochrane, *Journal of Finance* 2011. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01671.x — Supports time-varying discount-rate interpretation. Limitation: not a tactical cross-asset rule.
- **The Golden Dilemma** — Erb and Harvey, 2013. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2078535 — Supports caution about permanent gold relationships. Limitation: historical specification.
- Instrument definitions and methodology are drawn from the companion Federal Reserve, Treasury, FRED, and index-provider notes.

## Claim-to-source map

| Claim | Claim type | Source | Safe wording | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correlation is not causation | Empirical-method principle | Econometric identification | Treat common cause as alternative | Does not select cause |
| Gold-rate relations vary | Empirical relationship | Erb/Harvey and companion notes | Regime-dependent | No stable beta |
| Divergence is diagnostic | Practitioner heuristic | Editorial synthesis | A question, not a signal | Resolution direction unknown |

## Confidence decision

Instrument definitions: medium. Directional confirmation, divergence resolution, and lead/lag: low.
