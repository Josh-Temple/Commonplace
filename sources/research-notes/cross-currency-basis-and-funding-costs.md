# Research question

Why can actual FX funding costs differ from policy-rate differentials?

# Definitions

Funding currency, target currency, spot, forward, FX swap, carry, hedge, basis, gross and net exposure are kept distinct. Definitions follow the official or scholarly sources listed below.

# Institutional mechanisms

The note separates cash borrowing from derivatives-based funding, hedged investment from unhedged carry, and observed contract balances from inferred trading intent.

# Evidence reviewed

Primary emphasis is BIS, central-bank, IMF, Treasury/MOF and CFTC documentation, plus peer-reviewed or NBER research where the empirical proposition requires it.

# Claims supported

| Claim | Claim type | Source | Safe wording | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basis measures departure from a covered parity benchmark | Definition | BIS “Covered interest parity lost” | Define with a sign-convention warning | Quoting conventions differ |
| Bank balance-sheet costs can impede arbitrage | Institutional mechanism | BIS and Federal Reserve research on CIP deviations | Say constraints can sustain deviations | Attribution is not unique |

# Claims requiring conditional wording

Directional transmission to yen, equities, credit, gold or the broad dollar is a practitioner interpretation unless an episode-specific design identifies it. Low volatility may permit leverage; it does not reveal leverage.

# Measurement problems

CFTC futures omit much OTC and offshore activity and cannot reveal every trader's intent. Bank lending and BIS banking data have defined reporting populations. FX-swap and options data show contracts, not a unified net carry book. Dealer estimates can mix definitions. Gross versus net, hedged versus unhedged, institutional versus retail, offshore entities, netting and synthetic exposure prevent a precise real-time total.

# Competing explanations

Policy repricing, broad dollar moves, trade and portfolio flows, hedging, repatriation, intervention expectations, recession risk, collateral scarcity and general deleveraging can produce overlapping observations.

# Claims not supported

No current carry size, exact leverage, unwind probability, stable correlation, win rate, imminent event, or deterministic gold/equity direction is supported.

# Remaining uncertainty

The sources define mechanisms and bounded datasets but do not combine all cash and derivative exposures or identify every position's purpose. Episode attribution requires dated, episode-specific work.
