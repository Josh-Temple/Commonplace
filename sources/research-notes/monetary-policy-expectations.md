# Monetary-policy expectations — research note

## Purpose

Support FOMC mechanics, target ranges, communication, balance-sheet policy, and market-implied expectations.

## Evidence record

- **Federal Open Market Committee** — Federal Reserve Board. https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomc.htm — Supports the committee role, statements, minutes, projections, and meeting materials.
- **Policy Tools** — Federal Reserve Board. https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/policytools.htm — Supports policy-rate implementation and balance-sheet tools.
- **FedWatch Tool methodology/education** — CME Group. https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/interest-rates/cme-fedwatch-tool.html — Supports extracting probability-like distributions from Fed Funds futures. Limitation: exchange education and market-implied estimates, not forecasts or official Fed views.
- **FAQs: Federal Reserve Balance Sheet Developments** — Federal Reserve Board. https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/bst_fedsbalancesheet.htm — Supports balance-sheet reporting. Limitation: balance-sheet size alone is not a complete liquidity measure.

## Editorial limits

These sources support definitions and causal channels, not a stable trading coefficient or mechanical signal. Market reactions depend on expectations, starting conditions, risk premiums, positioning, and the shock's cause. URLs were selected from official or institutional publishers; no current-policy claim is preserved here.
