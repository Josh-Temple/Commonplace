# Yield curve and term premium — research note

## Purpose

Support maturity terminology, curve spreads, expectations, and model-dependent term-premium estimates.

## Evidence record

- **Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates** — U.S. Department of the Treasury. https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve — Official maturity-specific Treasury par yields. Limitation: par yields are not every security’s traded yield.
- **10-Year Treasury Constant Maturity Minus 2-Year Treasury Constant Maturity (T10Y2Y)** — Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, FRED. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/T10Y2Y — Supports the conventional 2s10s spread definition. Limitation: a spread alone hides movement in each leg.
- **Treasury Term Premia: 1961-Present** — Federal Reserve Bank of New York (Adrian, Crump, Moench model). https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/data_indicators/term-premia-tabs — Supports term premium as an estimated decomposition. Limitation: not directly observed; model estimates can differ.
- **The Yield Curve as a Leading Indicator** — Federal Reserve Bank of New York. https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/capital_markets/ycfaq.html — Supports probabilistic historical recession information. Limitation: not a fixed-timetable market-timing rule.

## Editorial limits

These sources support definitions and causal channels, not a stable trading coefficient or mechanical signal. Market reactions depend on expectations, starting conditions, risk premiums, positioning, and the shock's cause. URLs were selected from official or institutional publishers; no current-policy claim is preserved here.
