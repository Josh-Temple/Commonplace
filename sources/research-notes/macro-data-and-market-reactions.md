# Macro data and market reactions — research note

## Purpose

Support release definitions, schedules, revisions, and the distinction between data levels and surprises.

## Evidence record

- **Schedule of Releases for the Consumer Price Index** — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm — Official CPI release schedule and links.
- **Employment Situation** — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm — Supports payroll, unemployment, earnings, participation, and revision definitions.
- **Personal Income and Outlays** — U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis. https://www.bea.gov/news/current-releases — Supports PCE publication and revision context.
- **Gross Domestic Product** — U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis. https://www.bea.gov/data/gdp/gross-domestic-product — Supports GDP definition and estimates.
- **Manufacturing and Services ISM Report On Business** — Institute for Supply Management. https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-report-on-business/ — Supports diffusion-index survey context. Limitation: private survey and not an official output measure.
- **Treasury Auction Announcements and Results** — U.S. Department of the Treasury. https://treasurydirect.gov/auctions/announcements-data-results/ — Official auction terms/results. Limitation: interpretation of tails and bidder shares is practitioner analysis, not a guaranteed market signal.

## Editorial limits

These sources support definitions and causal channels, not a stable trading coefficient or mechanical signal. Market reactions depend on expectations, starting conditions, risk premiums, positioning, and the shock's cause. URLs were selected from official or institutional publishers; no current-policy claim is preserved here.
