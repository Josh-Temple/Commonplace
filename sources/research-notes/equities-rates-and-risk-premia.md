# Equities, rates, and risk premia — research note

## Purpose

Support present-value logic, equity risk premium concepts, and the conditional relation between rates and equities.

## Evidence record

- **Discount Rates** — John H. Cochrane, *Journal of Finance* 2011, DOI 10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01671.x. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01671.x — Supports the central role of time-varying discount rates in asset prices. Limitation: academic synthesis, not a tactical signal.
- **Valuation of the S&P 500** — S&P Dow Jones Indices methodology resources. https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/ — Supports index composition context. Limitation: index-provider material does not prove a rate sensitivity.
- **Corporate Bond Yield Spreads and the Business Cycle** — Federal Reserve research context via FRED ICE BofA spread series. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/categories/32297 — Supports observable credit-spread series. Limitation: vendor index methodology and composition matter.
- **The Equity Premium** — Rajnish Mehra and Edward C. Prescott, *Journal of Monetary Economics* 1985, DOI 10.1016/0304-3932(85)90061-3. — Supports the equity-premium concept, not a current expected-premium estimate.

## Editorial limits

These sources support definitions and causal channels, not a stable trading coefficient or mechanical signal. Market reactions depend on expectations, starting conditions, risk premiums, positioning, and the shock's cause. URLs were selected from official or institutional publishers; no current-policy claim is preserved here.
